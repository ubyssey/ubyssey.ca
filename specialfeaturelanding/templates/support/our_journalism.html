Journalism at The Ubyssey

OurJournalism

The Ubyssey is an independent, non-profit, public service student press covering the University of British Columbia community since 1918.

Here’s what it means to us

01

Independent

We say we are independent because we are an autonomous organization. We answer to the public, not to the AMS, UBC or another institution.

02

Non-Profit

The Ubyssey is published by The Ubyssey Publication Society, a registered non-profit organization under the Societies Act. All the money we collect goes right back into our journalism.

03

Public Service

We believe that journalism is a public service, and we centre that idea across all our sections.

04

Student Press

We are a student press, meaning we are directed and operated by students. Our core readership is the student body, who funds our journalism through an annual fee.

05

UBC Community

Our community defines the scope of what we do. It is our duty to ask the questions and report the answers that matter to the people at our university.

06

Since 1918

We’ve been doing this since 1918. Our first issue was published on October 17, 1918.

Trust is something we practise.

Our standards are reflected in the way we report, correct mistakes and listen to readers.
