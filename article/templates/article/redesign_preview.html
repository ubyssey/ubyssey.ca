The story begins at a candlelight vigil, where students and community members gathered beneath the trees to reflect on four years of war. Speakers described the distance between campus life and the daily reality faced by families in Ukraine.

Organizers said the event was intended to make space for grief while also asking what sustained solidarity can look like. Music, testimony and a minute of silence brought together people whose relationships to the conflict were deeply personal.

As the crowd grew, attendees placed candles along the pavement and shared stories about relatives abroad. Several speakers connected the vigil to broader questions about displacement, international students and the responsibilities of public institutions.
