“Why does snow disappear overnight?” — Aria, 12

When temperatures drop below 0°C, water vapor condenses. Skipping the liquid stage, these would-be raindrops freeze and become the beloved substance we know as “snow.”

However, like all good things in life, snow will leave you...

Reportedly, these snow particles ask themselves existential questions such as “Why are we sticking together?”

“FOMO” is real, even for snowflakes!

Some lucky snowflakes get to be felt on one’s skin, and others’ dreams are dashed...

“Can my dog understand me?” — Mindy, 10

Thanks for asking, Mindy! Did you know that dogs were selectively bred from wolves?

Dogs have excellent hearing. They detect sounds that are much quieter than humans can.

So can dogs understand us? Yes! They are our wolf familiars, the sneaky snitches we keep near.
